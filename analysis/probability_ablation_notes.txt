FRAUD PROBABILITY ABLATION BENCHMARK
========================================================================

Purpose: controlled feature ablation and risk_score direction test.
This experiment does not select a production model.

VARIANTS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
baseline: 21 features; risk_transform=none
cleaned: 18 features; risk_transform=none
inverted_risk: 18 features; risk_transform=invert
no_risk_score: 17 features; risk_transform=none
minimal_evidence: 15 features; risk_transform=none

CALIBRATION
- Sigmoid/Platt calibration fitted only on chronological validation.
- Test set is used only for final evaluation.

METRIC EXTREMES
------------------------------------------------------------------------
roc_auc (max): baseline / hist_gradient_boosting = 0.987179
pr_auc (max): baseline / hist_gradient_boosting = 0.998654
brier (min): inverted_risk / hist_gradient_boosting = 0.027748
log_loss (min): baseline / hist_gradient_boosting = 0.088065
ece_10 (min): no_risk_score / hist_gradient_boosting = 0.014837

INTERPRETATION
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Compare metrics across variants rather than relying on one metric alone.
The inverted risk_score experiment is a diagnostic test of direction, not permission to redefine the upstream score without verifying its semantics.
The high historical fraud prevalence remains a population/calibration limitation.
The benchmark is case-level: label=1 means historical case outcome was confirmed fraud.
