TIGERGRAPH AGENTIC FRAUD INVESTIGATION — BENCHMARK DEEP ANALYSIS
========================================================================

This analysis produces observed signals, derived signals, pattern candidates and historical support.
It is NOT a final fraud decision engine.

Benchmark cases analyzed: 20
Historical cases loaded: 5,565

Temporal leakage controls:
- Evidence is restricted to transaction activity at or before case opening.
- Historical closed cases are restricted to closed_at <= case opening.
- Future transactions after case opening are not used as evidence.

Device profile:
- Exact profile = DeviceInfo + id_30 + id_31 + id_33.
- id_15 and id_23 are not part of the core profile.
- Partial/common profiles are not treated as definitive shared-device proof.

Card identity:
- case_pack card_id values such as C12382-K1/K2 are preserved exactly.
- Raw transaction card fingerprints are generated only from card1..card6.
- No K1/K2 mapping is inferred.

Fraud probability:
- No fraud probability is calculated by this script.
- risk_score is retained as an input signal only.
- The eventual investigation agent must combine graph evidence, historical evidence, pattern detection, policy and simulated verification.

Pattern candidate counts:
- card_testing_candidate: 1/20
- cnp_candidate: 3/20
- cnp_new_device_candidate: 11/20
- out_of_region_candidate: 0/20
- account_takeover_candidate: 5/20
- shared_device_candidate: 7/20
- repeated_abuse_candidate: 12/20

Historical support:
- Cases with >=1 prior confirmed fraud case: 14
- Cases with >=1 prior cleared case: 12

Interpretation rule:
Historical cases and pattern candidates are supporting evidence, not automatic proof of current fraud.
Undocumented coordinated or repeated abuse should remain discoverable instead of being forced into one of the documented patterns.
